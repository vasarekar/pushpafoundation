Our mission

Building strong, self-reliant communities

Pushpa Foundation is dedicated to empowering rural communities by fostering self-reliance, education, and sustainable growth. Through community-led initiatives, we nurture local leadership, promote equitable access to resources, and create pathways for lasting social and economic transformation.

What we do

Our Services

01

Rural Educational Infrastructure Support

We strengthen rural learning environments by building essential educational infrastructure. We have set up computer labs, introduced digital literacy, and provided dedicated rest rooms for girls.

02

Water Conservation

We have planted over 100 trees and provided water containers to communities in need, while expanding efforts to include rainwater harvesting, efficient irrigation, and awareness programs.

03

Emergency Relief

We provide food and essential supplies to families impacted by floods and financial assistance to families of farmers who have tragically lost their lives to suicide.

04

Community Care & Essential Support

We provide warm sweaters to orphanage children and donate sarees to women facing financial hardship, strengthening communities with warmth, respect, and hope.
